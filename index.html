THE GRIMOIRE PRO TIPS

Welcome to your digital manor engine.

  • WakeLock Active: SomniaScourings uses the WakeLock API. Your screen will NOT go to sleep while you are reviewing your tasks.
  • Manor Dynamics: A pristine sanctuary is vital. Use the tabs to assign cleaning zones to specific areas of your home!
  • Defend the Realm: Keep the local weather at bay by tracking your HVAC filter changes in the Maintenance section. Blocked out directly on your calendar colors.
  • Private Workshop: Everything lives exclusively in your device's IndexedDB. Export your data securely using the Vault tab!

🤖 Pro Tip: Awakening the Castellan

The AI Castellan can auto-generate seasonal maintenance checklists based on your specific manor needs.
1. Go to aistudio.google.com and sign in.
2. Click "Get API key".
3. Click AI Castellan in the top menu.
4. Paste your key and click Save.
Example Prompt: "Generate a 30-day maintenance schedule for a Gothic manor."
